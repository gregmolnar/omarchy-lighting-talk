01 — What is it

What Omarchy is

  • Beautiful, fun & agentic Linux by DHH
  • Malleable and agent friendly
  • Keyboard first. Hyprland tiling
  • Themed as one system. 22 themes; one command reskins terminal, editor, bar and lock screen.
  • Extendable with plugins. The directory already lists over 3,000 — omarchy-plugin-add and it's in your menu.
The distro point is the one that matters — this is config, not a fork. Nothing here locks you in.

03 — Machine support

It runs on potato machines

  • Almost anything. A 2011 ThinkPad X220 with 2 GB of RAM is a documented target.
  • Vintage Intel Macs. A second life for hardware Apple stopped shipping updates for.
  • Apple Silicon. In progress — a dedicated Omarchy M team is on it.
  • Vendor fixes. Framework, ThinkPad, XPS, ROG, Surface, Nvidia.

omarchy.org/manual/mac-support

04 — In practice

Install

  • Grab the ISO. Checksum and GPG signature sit next to it.
  • Write it to a USB stick, boot, answer five questions.
  • Full disk or dual boot with Windows; unattended installs too.
  • Fast. 35 seconds on quick machines, under two minutes on most.
  • Or try it first. Signed VM apps for Apple Silicon Macs and Windows 10/11.
